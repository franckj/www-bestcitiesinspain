## Competitor Weaknesses Identified
- No dedicated domain for this keyword cluster — all results are blog posts on general travel sites
- Zero purpose-built seasonal content ("best cities spain december" etc.)
- No audience-specific content ("for families", "for food lovers")
- Shallow per-city content on existing rankers (200-400 words per city in listicles)
- Weak affiliate integration on ranking pages

## Known Decisions / Constraints
- No /cities/ prefix — go straight to /barcelona/, /madrid/ etc. (domain already signals topic)
- /guides/ URL for long-form content (displayed as "Guides" in nav)
- Start with 8 cities, expand based on traffic data
- Voice is practical-authoritative, not opinionated-insider (different from ilovecatalonia)
- Day 1 city pages are ~600 words each — structured for expansion
- Guide articles come in Phase 2 (week 2+)
