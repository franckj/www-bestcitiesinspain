## Is Granada worth visiting?

Yes, if the Alhambra is on your list. The **Alhambra, the Generalife and the Albaicín** form one UNESCO World Heritage site, listed in 1984 and extended in 1994. UNESCO describes the Alhambra and Generalife as an exceptional testimony to Muslim Spain in the 13th to 15th centuries ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/314/)).

Granada is a smaller city than Seville, with about 233,500 people ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granada)). It suits you if you like history, hill walks and long tapas evenings. It suits you less if you can't book the Alhambra ahead or don't like steep cobbled streets: the Albaicín sits on a hill north of the Darro river ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albaic%C3%ADn)).

## Alhambra tickets

Buy from the [official ticket site](https://tickets.alhambra-patronato.es/en/), by phone on +34 858 889 002, at the machines on site, or at the ticket office for the same day if any are left ([Alhambra FAQ](https://www.alhambra-patronato.es/en/visit/faq)). Prices below are from the [official price list](https://www.alhambra-patronato.es/en/visit/opening-hours-and-prices), checked 14 October 2026.

| Ticket | Includes | Price |
|---|---|---|
| General Day Tour | Alcazaba, Nasrid Palaces, Generalife | €22.27 |
| Gardens Day Tour | Generalife gardens and palace, no Nasrid Palaces | €12.73 |
| Night Tour to Nasrid Palaces | Nasrid Palaces only | €12.73 |
| Night Tour to Gardens | Gardens and Generalife | €8.48 |
| Alhambra Experiences | Nasrid Palaces at night, Alcazaba and gardens by day | €22.27 |
| Rodríguez-Acosta Foundation and Monumental Complex | General visit plus the Foundation | €25.45 |
| Dobla de Oro General | Alhambra plus the Andalusian monuments in the city | €30.48 |

The route for each ticket is on the [types of visit](https://www.alhambra-patronato.es/en/visit/types-of-visit) page. Children under 12 go free but still need a ticket issued with the adults'. Reduced prices need official proof on the day ([prices page](https://www.alhambra-patronato.es/en/visit/opening-hours-and-prices)).

**The Nasrid Palaces have a fixed entry time.** Capacity is **300 people every half an hour**. If you're not at the Nasrid Palaces entrance at the time on your ticket, you won't be allowed in. The rest of the site can be visited at any time during opening hours ([Alhambra FAQ](https://www.alhambra-patronato.es/en/visit/faq)). The slot is set when you buy and can't be changed ([Spain.info](https://www.spain.info/en/top/how-to-buy-tickets-visit-alhambra/)).

**How far ahead:** tickets go on sale up to **three months in advance**, and there is "no guarantee of availability" ([Spain.info](https://www.spain.info/en/top/how-to-buy-tickets-visit-alhambra/)). The Alhambra says it may limit sales and recommends buying in advance ([prices page](https://www.alhambra-patronato.es/en/visit/opening-hours-and-prices)). We couldn't find an official figure for how many weeks ahead dates sell out.

**Other rules:** tickets are personal; bring the passport or ID card used for the booking. The name can be changed up to the day before. Date and time can't be changed, and purchases are final ([Alhambra FAQ](https://www.alhambra-patronato.es/en/visit/faq)).

**Opening hours:** daytime visits run 08:30–18:00 from 15 October to 31 March and 08:30–20:00 from 1 April to 14 October. Closed 25 December and 1 January ([official hours](https://www.alhambra-patronato.es/en/visit/opening-hours-and-prices)). If official tickets are gone, guided tours that include tickets (below) are another way in.

## What to see

- **The Alhambra.** Alcazaba fortress, Nasrid Palaces and the Generalife gardens. The general visit takes around three hours ([Alhambra FAQ](https://www.alhambra-patronato.es/en/visit/faq)). See the ticket section above.
- **The Albaicín and the Mirador de San Nicolás.** The old Moorish quarter, part of the UNESCO site. UNESCO says its architecture and street plan are the most remarkable survival of Andalusí culture ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/314/)). The square by the church of San Nicolás is a lookout with views of the Alhambra ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albaic%C3%ADn)).
- **Sacromonte.** Hillside cave houses facing the Alhambra, home to Granada's Romani community since after 1492. The zambra form of flamenco originated here ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sacromonte)).
- **Royal Chapel (Capilla Real).** Burial place of the Catholic Monarchs, Isabella I of Castile and Ferdinand of Aragon ([Capilla Real](https://capillarealgranada.com/)). Closed on Good Friday, 25 December and 1 January; check [visit times](https://capillarealgranada.com/visita-cultural/) before you go.
- **Granada Cathedral.** Next to the Royal Chapel. Visits are not possible during services and times can change without notice; see the [official prices and hours](https://catedraldegranada.com/precios-horarios-de-la-visita/).
- **Andalusian monuments.** El Bañuelo, Corral del Carbón, Casa Horno de Oro and Palacio de Dar al-Horra share one ticket at **€8.48**, free on Sundays ([official prices](https://www.alhambra-patronato.es/en/visit/opening-hours-and-prices), checked 14 October 2026).
- **Tapas.** In most bars a free tapa comes with each drink. The tourism office lists areas such as Calle Navas, Bib-Rambla, the Albaicín and Realejo ([Granada tourism office](https://turismo.granada.org/en/what-do/tapas-granada)).

## How many days

**2 days.** Day one: the Alhambra, built around your Nasrid Palaces slot. Day two: the Albaicín, Sacromonte, the Royal Chapel and the Cathedral, then tapas. Add a third day for a trip to the Alpujarra villages. Combining Granada with Seville and Madrid? See our [itineraries](/itineraries/).

## Getting there

| From | How | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Madrid | Renfe high-speed train, 4 direct a day | 3 h 23 to 3 h 41 (Renfe timetable, 21 Oct 2026) |
| Barcelona | Renfe high-speed train | From 6 h 25 min |
| Seville | Renfe Avant, direct | About 2 h 40 min (estimate) |

Madrid and Barcelona times come from [Spain.info](https://www.spain.info/en/route/explore-spain-on-high-speed-trains/). The high-speed line into Granada opened on 26 June 2019 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antequera%E2%80%93Granada_high-speed_rail_line)). The Seville time is a timetable estimate from booking sites, not an official figure; check [Renfe](https://www.renfe.com/) for your date.

**By air:** Federico García Lorca Granada-Jaén Airport (GRX) is about 15 km west of the city ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federico_Garc%C3%ADa_Lorca_Granada_Airport)).

## Where to stay

- **Around the Cathedral and Plaza Nueva:** near the Royal Chapel and the tapas areas the tourism office lists around Bib-Rambla and Calle Navas.
- **The Albaicín:** views of the Alhambra and the San Nicolás lookout on your doorstep, but steep streets.

## When to go

Spring and autumn. Granada sits at 738 m. Average daily highs at Granada Airport (AEMET, 1991–2020) are **13.0 °C in January, 20.6 °C in April, 34.8 °C in July and 23.2 °C in October**, and January nights average 0.3 °C ([Wikipedia, from AEMET data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granada)). Pack layers in winter. Note the Alhambra's shorter winter hours, 08:30–18:00 from 15 October. See [the best time to visit Spain](/best-time-to-visit-spain/).
