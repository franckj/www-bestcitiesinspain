## Is Barcelona worth visiting?

Yes, for most first-time visitors to Spain. Barcelona is the capital of Catalonia, on the Mediterranean. Seven buildings by Antoni Gaudí (1852–1926) in and near the city are a single UNESCO World Heritage site, first listed in 1984 ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/320/)).

It suits you if you like architecture, walking and a city by the sea. It suits you less if you don't like booking ahead: the Sagrada Família and Park Güell both sell timed tickets.

For a deeper Barcelona and Catalonia guide, see [I Love Catalonia](https://ilovecatalonia.com/).

## What to see

- **Sagrada Família:** construction began on 19 March 1882, and Gaudí took over the design in 1883. The basilica was consecrated in 2010 and is still being built ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sagrada_Fam%C3%ADlia)). Gaudí's Nativity façade and crypt are part of the UNESCO site. Official tickets: €26, €30 with a guided tour, €36 with a tower, €40 with a guide and a tower ([Sagrada Família](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/tickets-individuals), checked 14 October 2026).
- **Park Güell:** Gaudí's park, also on the UNESCO list. Ticketed entry runs in time slots from 9:30 to 19:30, and you must enter within 30 minutes of your slot. General ticket €18, ages 7–12 and over-65s €13.50, under-7s free. Tickets are sold online only ([Park Güell](https://parkguell.barcelona/en/planning-your-visit/prices-and-times), checked 14 October 2026).
- **Casa Batlló and Casa Milà (La Pedrera):** two Gaudí apartment buildings, both part of the UNESCO site ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/320/)). We haven't listed their prices: check their own sites before you go.
- **Gothic Quarter (Barri Gòtic):** the historic centre of the old city, between La Rambla and Via Laietana. It has remains of a Roman wall and temple, the Cathedral of Santa Eulàlia and squares such as Plaça Sant Jaume, Plaça del Rei and Plaça Reial ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_Quarter,_Barcelona)).
- **Museu Picasso:** at Carrer Montcada 15–23. From 29 September to 29 March it's open Tuesday to Sunday 10:00–19:00. Collection plus temporary exhibition: €12 online, €13 at the ticket office ([Museu Picasso](https://museupicassobcn.cat/en), checked 14 October 2026).
- **La Boqueria market:** the food market at La Rambla 91, open Monday to Saturday 8:00–20:30 ([Mercat de la Boqueria](https://www.boqueria.barcelona/), checked 14 October 2026).
- **Palau de la Música Catalana and Hospital de Sant Pau:** two Modernista buildings by Lluís Domènech i Montaner, a separate UNESCO site listed in 1997 ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/804/)).

## How many days

Three days covers the main sights:

- **Day 1:** the Sagrada Família, then Casa Batlló and Casa Milà on Passeig de Gràcia.
- **Day 2:** the Gothic Quarter, La Boqueria and the Picasso Museum.
- **Day 3:** Park Güell in the morning, then the Palau de la Música or Sant Pau.

Add a day for Montserrat, which coach tours reach from the city.

## Getting there

- **From Madrid:** direct high-speed trains take 3 h 02 to 3 h 47 from Puerta de Atocha ([Renfe timetable](/getting-around-spain/), 21 October 2026). Ouigo, one of the operators, runs from Madrid Puerta de Atocha to Barcelona Sants, from €9 ([Ouigo](https://www.ouigo.com/en/madrid-barcelona-train-es)).
- **From other cities:** Seville from 5 hours 18 minutes, Málaga from 6 hours, Granada from 6 hours 25 minutes ([Spain.info](https://www.spain.info/en/route/explore-spain-on-high-speed-trains/)).
- **From the airport:** Metro line L9 Sud serves terminals T1 and T2 and reaches the city centre in about 32 minutes, every 7 minutes. Standard single and T-casual tickets aren't valid at the airport stations: buy the Airport Ticket ([TMB](https://www.tmb.cat/en/visit-barcelona/public-transport/metro-airport)).

More on trains between cities in [getting around Spain](/getting-around-spain/).

## Where to stay

- **Eixample:** the grid district planned by Ildefons Cerdà, built in the 19th and early 20th centuries. Casa Batlló and Casa Milà are on its main avenue, Passeig de Gràcia, and the Sagrada Família is also in the district ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eixample)).
- **Gothic Quarter:** the old town between La Rambla and Via Laietana ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothic_Quarter,_Barcelona)), so La Boqueria and the cathedral are on foot.

More options in [where to stay](/where-to-stay/).

## When to go

Barcelona is mild in winter and warm in summer. At the airport weather station, average highs are 21 °C in May and 22 °C in October, against 28 °C in July and 28.5 °C in August. January highs average 13.6 °C. October is the wettest month, with 91 mm on average (AEMET 1981–2010 normals, [AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/en/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=0076&k=cat)).
