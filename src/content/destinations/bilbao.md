Bilbao has 347,342 people (2024) and about a million in the metropolitan area. It was founded by charter in 1300 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilbao)).

## Is Bilbao worth visiting?

Yes, for most first-time visitors heading north. It has the Guggenheim, and the old town (Casco Viejo) is full of pintxo bars. [San Sebastián](/destinations/san-sebastian/) is about 1 hour 20 minutes away by coach, so it's easy to see both.

It suits art and architecture fans, food lovers and anyone using the Basque coast as a base. It suits less well if you want beaches in town or warm, dry weather: the airport averages rain on 124 days a year ([AEMET, 1981–2010 normals](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=1082&k=pva)).

## What to see

- **Guggenheim Museum Bilbao.** Designed by Frank Gehry and opened on 18 October 1997. Richard Serra's *The Matter of Time* is its only permanent installation ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Museum_Bilbao)). On 14 October 2026 adult tickets were €12, reduced €6, under-18s free, with an audio guide included. Open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–19:00, closed on most Mondays ([Guggenheim Bilbao](https://www.guggenheim-bilbao.eus/en/plan-your-visit)).
- **Puppy.** Jeff Koons's 12.4 m terrier, covered in about 38,000 flowering plants, has stood outside the museum since it opened in 1997 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puppy_(sculpture))).
- **Casco Viejo and the Seven Streets.** The oldest part of the old town is seven streets: Somera, Artekale, Tendería, Belostikale, Carnicería Vieja, Barrenkale and Barrenkale Barrena ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casco_Viejo)).
- **Santiago Cathedral.** A Gothic church of the 14th and 15th centuries, dedicated to Saint James as a stop on the northern Camino de Santiago. It became a cathedral in 1955 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilbao_Cathedral)).
- **Plaza Nueva.** The arcaded neoclassical square of the old town, inaugurated in 1851 ([Tourism Euskadi](https://tourism.euskadi.eus/en/cultural-heritage/plaza-nueva-square/webtur00-content/en/)).
- **Mercado de la Ribera.** The covered food market on the river next to the Casco Viejo, opened on 22 August 1929 to a design by Pedro Ispizua ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercado_de_la_Ribera)).
- **Bilbao Fine Arts Museum.** Opened in 1914 in Doña Casilda Iturrizar Park, with works by El Greco, Zurbarán, Goya, Gauguin, Mary Cassatt and Francis Bacon ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilbao_Fine_Arts_Museum)). A combined ticket with the Guggenheim costs €16 for adults ([Guggenheim Bilbao](https://www.guggenheim-bilbao.eus/en/plan-your-visit), checked 14 October 2026).
- **Artxanda funicular.** Running since 1915, it climbs about 770 m in roughly 3 minutes from a station near the Zubizuri bridge, within walking distance of the Guggenheim ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artxanda_Funicular)).
- **Vizcaya Bridge.** Built in 1893 by Alberto Palacio, it links Portugalete and Las Arenas (Getxo) at the mouth of the estuary. It became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2006, and lifts take visitors up to the top walkway ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vizcaya_Bridge)).

## How many days

Two days for the city. Day one: the Guggenheim, then walk along the river to the Zubizuri bridge and ride the Artxanda funicular. Day two: the Casco Viejo, Santiago Cathedral, the Ribera market and Plaza Nueva, with the Fine Arts Museum if you have time. A third day works for the Vizcaya Bridge, a day trip to [San Sebastián](/destinations/san-sebastian/), or a tour to San Juan de Gaztelugatxe.

## Getting there

| From | How | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Madrid Chamartín | Renfe, 2 direct a day | 5 h 10 to 5 h 14 (Renfe timetable, 21 Oct 2026) |
| Barcelona Sants | Renfe Alvia, 1 direct a day | About 6 h 55 min |
| San Sebastián | Alsa coach, more than 12 a day | About 1 h 20 min |

Madrid times come from Renfe's published timetable for 21 October 2026 (see [getting around Spain](/getting-around-spain/)). The Barcelona time is an estimate from a booking site, not from Renfe directly: [Everyrail](https://everyrail.com/trains/barcelona-to-bilbao/). Everyrail reports that on some weekends until 12–13 December 2026, engineering works replace the Logroño–Miranda de Ebro section of the Barcelona route with a coach. Coach details are from [Alsa](https://www.alsa.com/pt/autocarro/bilbau-san-sebastian). Check [renfe.com](https://www.renfe.com/) before booking.

**Airport.** Bilbao airport is about 12 km from the centre. Bus A3247 runs to the Intermodal bus station every 15–20 minutes from 06:00 to midnight, stopping at Gran Vía 79, Plaza Moyua and Alameda Recalde. A taxi costs €27–30 ([Spain.info](https://www.spain.info/en/aeroplane/bilbao-airport/)). See [getting around Spain](/getting-around-spain/).

## Where to stay

- **Casco Viejo:** for the old town's pintxo bars, Plaza Nueva and the Ribera market on your doorstep.
- **Abando:** the city centre on the left bank. It has the Gran Vía, Bilbao-Abando railway station and, in its northern part, the Guggenheim ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abando)). Handy if you arrive by train.

More on choosing a base in our guide to [where to stay in Spain](/where-to-stay/).

## When to go

Bilbao has a mild, wet climate. At the airport, average highs range from 13.4 °C in January to 26.0 °C in August. November is the wettest month (147 mm) and July the driest (50 mm) ([AEMET, 1981–2010](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=1082&k=pva)).

May to September is the driest stretch. The city's main festival, Aste Nagusia, lasts nine days from the first Saturday after 15 August ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aste_Nagusia)). See [the best time to visit Spain](/best-time-to-visit-spain/).
