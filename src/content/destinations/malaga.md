## Is Málaga worth visiting?

Yes. Málaga is Spain's sixth-largest city, with 592,346 people in 2024 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A1laga)). Most of what you'll want to see sits within a short walk of the Alcazaba: a Moorish fortress, a Roman theatre at its foot, a Renaissance cathedral and the Picasso Museum. Picasso was born here in 1881 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pablo_Picasso)).

It suits you if you want Andalusian history and a strong art museum in one compact centre, and a base for trips to Granada or the Caminito del Rey. You might skip it if you've only got a few days in Andalusia: Seville and Granada have the bigger monuments.

## What to see

- **Alcazaba:** a palace-fortress begun in the 11th century and changed up to the 14th, and one of the best-preserved in Spain ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcazaba_of_M%C3%A1laga)). Entry is €7, or €10 with Gibralfaro Castle. It's free for everyone on Sundays from 14:00. Open 9:00–20:00 from 1 April to 31 October and 9:00–18:00 from 1 November to 31 March, with last entry 1 hour before closing ([Alcazaba y Gibralfaro](https://alcazabaygibralfaro.malaga.eu/es/visitas/horarios-y-tarifas/), checked 14 October 2026).
- **Gibralfaro Castle:** on top of the 130 m hill above the Alcazaba, linked to it by a walled corridor ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibralfaro)). It's on the €10 combined ticket.
- **Roman theatre:** built in the early 1st century AD and found in 1951 during gardening works. Entry is free. Open Tuesday to Saturday 10:00–18:00, Sundays and holidays 10:00–16:00, and closed on Mondays ([Junta de Andalucía](https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/aaiicc/enclaves/enclave-arqueologico-teatro-romano-de-malaga), checked 14 October 2026).
- **Museo Picasso Málaga:** opened in 2003 in the Buenavista Palace, with 285 works given by Picasso's family to start the collection ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museo_Picasso_M%C3%A1laga)). A full ticket is €13 and includes an audio guide on your phone. It's free on Sundays for the last 2 hours. Open every day: 10:00–19:00 from March to June and in September and October, 10:00–20:00 in July and August, and 10:00–18:00 from November to February ([Museo Picasso Málaga](https://www.museopicassomalaga.org/en/visita), checked 14 October 2026).
- **Picasso's birthplace (Museo Casa Natal):** the house on Plaza de la Merced where he was born, now a museum run by the city ([Wikipedia, Spanish](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Museo_Casa_Natal_Picasso)). We couldn't confirm its current prices.
- **Málaga Cathedral:** Renaissance, built between 1528 and 1782 to plans by Diego de Siloé ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A1laga_Cathedral)). Entry is €10 ([Catedral de Málaga](https://malagacatedral.com/visita-cultural/tarifas-y-horarios/), checked 14 October 2026). Dress modestly.
- **Atarazanas Market:** the central market, built between 1876 and 1879 on the site of a Nasrid shipyard. Only the marble gateway of the shipyard survives ([Wikipedia, Spanish](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercado_de_Atarazanas)).
- **Centre Pompidou Málaga:** the first branch of the Paris Centre Pompidou outside France, in a glass cube on the port, opened in 2015 ([Wikipedia, Spanish](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centro_Pompidou_de_M%C3%A1laga)).

## How many days

**2 days** works: the Alcazaba, Gibralfaro and the Roman theatre on day one, and the cathedral, the Picasso Museum and the market on day two. **3 days** lets you add a day trip. Renfe's Avant train reaches Granada in about 1 h 22 min, with 3 direct trains on the day we checked ([Renfe open data](https://data.renfe.com/dataset/horarios-de-alta-velocidad-larga-distancia-y-media-distancia), 21 October 2026). ## Getting there

| From | Train | Time | Direct trains |
|---|---|---|---|
| Madrid Puerta de Atocha | Renfe AVE / AVLO | 2 h 44 min – 3 h 15 min | 12 |
| Barcelona Sants | Renfe AVE | 6 h 41 min | 1 |
| Granada | Renfe Avant | 1 h 22 min – 1 h 23 min | 3 |

Times and counts are from [Renfe's open timetable data](https://data.renfe.com/dataset/horarios-de-alta-velocidad-larga-distancia-y-media-distancia) for Wednesday 21 October 2026 (downloaded 14 October 2026). Trains arrive at Málaga María Zambrano. [Iryo](https://iryo.eu/en/destinations/train-to-madrid) also runs Madrid–Málaga. More in [getting around Spain](/getting-around-spain/).

**By air:** Málaga–Costa del Sol Airport is 8 km southwest of the city ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C3%A1laga_Airport)). Cercanías line C-1 stops at the airport and runs to María Zambrano and Centro-Alameda in the city ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cercan%C3%ADas_M%C3%A1laga)).

## Where to stay

- **Centro Histórico:** the old town, a walk from the Alcazaba, the cathedral and the Picasso Museum.
- **Near María Zambrano station:** handy if you arrive by train or plan day trips, since the AVE, the Avant and the airport line all stop there.

## When to go

Málaga is mild in winter and hot in summer. The average high is 16.8 °C in January and 30.8 °C in August, and July has almost no rain ([AEMET normals for Málaga Airport, 1981–2010](https://www.aemet.es/es/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=6155A&k=and)). Spring and autumn are the most comfortable for walking up to Gibralfaro. See [the best time to visit Spain](/best-time-to-visit-spain/).

**Holy Week** (Semana Santa) is the main event: 45 processions by 42 brotherhoods, declared a Fiesta of International Tourist Interest in 1965 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Week_in_M%C3%A1laga)). The city also holds a street fair every August, the [Feria de Agosto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feria_de_M%C3%A1laga).
