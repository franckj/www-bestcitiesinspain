## Is Madrid worth visiting?

Yes, for most first-time visitors to Spain. Madrid is the capital, and its three big art museums sit close together along the Paseo del Prado. That avenue and Retiro Park were listed together as a UNESCO World Heritage site in 2021, as "Paseo del Prado and Buen Retiro, a landscape of Arts and Sciences" ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1618/)).

It suits you if you like museums, long meals and walking between neighbourhoods. It's also the hub of Spain's high-speed rail network, so it works as a base. It suits you less if you want the sea: Madrid is inland, and summers are hot.

## What to see

- **Museo del Prado:** opened on 10 November 1819. It holds about 8,600 paintings and over 700 sculptures, with Velázquez's *Las Meninas*, Goya's *Third of May 1808* and *Black Paintings*, and Bosch's *Garden of Earthly Delights*. Open Monday to Saturday 10:00–20:00, Sundays and holidays 10:00–19:00. General entry €15, reduced €7.50. Free Monday to Saturday 18:00–20:00 and Sundays 17:00–19:00 ([esMadrid](https://www.esmadrid.com/en/tourist-information/museo-del-prado), checked 14 October 2026). We couldn't load the museum's own site to confirm: check [museodelprado.es](https://www.museodelprado.es/en) before you go.
- **Royal Palace (Palacio Real):** run by Patrimonio Nacional. From October to March it's open Monday to Saturday 10:00–18:00 and Sunday 10:00–16:00; from April to September, Monday to Saturday until 19:00. Last entry is an hour before closing. Self-guided €18 (reduced €9), guided €26 ([Patrimonio Nacional](https://tickets.patrimonionacional.es/en/tickets/palacio-real-de-madrid), checked 14 October 2026). EU and Latin American citizens and residents get in free Monday to Thursday, 16:00–18:00 in winter and 17:00–19:00 in summer, self-guided only ([Patrimonio Nacional](https://www.patrimonionacional.es/en/tarifa-gratuita-palacio-real-de-madrid)).
- **Museo Reina Sofía:** modern and contemporary art, home of Picasso's *Guernica* (1937), which moved there from the Prado in 1992 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guernica_(Picasso))). Closed on Tuesdays. General entry €12 ([Reina Sofía](https://www.museoreinasofia.es/en/visit), checked 14 October 2026). Free entry runs on weekday evenings: check the times on the museum's page.
- **Retiro Park (Jardines del Buen Retiro):** 120 hectares, a remnant of the 17th-century Buen Retiro Palace, and the largest part of the UNESCO site ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1618/)). The Reina Sofía runs two free exhibition spaces in the park, the Palacio de Velázquez and the Palacio de Cristal. The Palacio de Cristal is temporarily closed for building work ([Reina Sofía](https://www.museoreinasofia.es/en/visit), checked 14 October 2026).
- **Plaza Mayor:** the arcaded main square, completed in 1619 and rebuilt by Juan de Villanueva after a fire in 1790. It measures 129 by 94 metres ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plaza_Mayor,_Madrid)).
- **Templo de Debod:** an Egyptian temple begun in the early 2nd century BC. Egypt gave it to Spain after Spain helped save temples from the Aswan Dam flooding, and it opened in Madrid's Cuartel de la Montaña Park in 1972. Free entry, closed on Mondays; booking is recommended ([esMadrid](https://www.esmadrid.com/en/tourist-information/templo-de-debod)).

**Day trips.** Toledo is about 30 minutes away by high-speed train ([Spain.info](https://www.spain.info/en/route/explore-spain-on-high-speed-trains/)). Its old town is a UNESCO World Heritage site, listed in 1986 ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/379/)). Segovia's old town and Roman aqueduct were listed in 1985 ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/311/)). Coach tours combine both in one day.

## How many days

Three days covers the main sights without rushing:

- **Day 1:** the Prado in the morning, Retiro Park in the afternoon.
- **Day 2:** the Royal Palace, Plaza Mayor and the old centre; Templo de Debod late in the day.
- **Day 3:** the Reina Sofía, then a neighbourhood of your choice.

Add a fourth day for Toledo or Segovia. Book the Royal Palace for a fixed day if you can: tickets go on sale up to 90 days ahead ([Patrimonio Nacional](https://tickets.patrimonionacional.es/en/tickets/palacio-real-de-madrid)).

## Getting there

- **From Barcelona:** direct high-speed trains take 3 h 02 to 3 h 47 to Puerta de Atocha ([Renfe timetable](/getting-around-spain/), 21 October 2026). Ouigo, one of the operators, goes from Barcelona Sants to Madrid Puerta de Atocha, from €9 ([Ouigo](https://www.ouigo.com/en/madrid-barcelona-train-es)).
- **From other cities:** Seville 2 h 38 to 3 h 09, Málaga 2 h 44 to 3 h 15, Granada 3 h 23 to 3 h 41 and Valencia 1 h 54 to 2 h 05 by direct train ([Renfe timetable](/getting-around-spain/), 21 October 2026).
- **From the airport:** Metro line 8 runs from Madrid-Barajas to Nuevos Ministerios, about 12 minutes from T1–T3 and 20 minutes from T4 ([esMadrid](https://www.esmadrid.com/en/madrid-metro)). Metro tickets need a €3 airport supplement ([Aena](https://www.aena.es/en/adolfo-suarez-madrid-barajas/getting-there/underground.html)). The Airport Express bus (route 203) runs 24 hours between the terminals and Atocha (Cibeles at night). It takes about 30 minutes to Terminal 1 and costs €5.10 ([esMadrid](https://www.esmadrid.com/en/airport-express-shuttle), checked 14 October 2026).

More on trains between cities in [getting around Spain](/getting-around-spain/).

## Where to stay

- **Sol and the old centre:** around Puerta del Sol and Plaza Mayor, within walking distance of the Royal Palace.
- **Barrio de las Letras:** between Calle de la Cruz and the Paseo del Prado, and between Carrera de San Jerónimo and Calle de Atocha ([Wikipedia, in Spanish](https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barrio_de_las_Letras)). Its eastern edge is the Paseo del Prado, where the Prado museum stands.

## When to go

Spring and autumn are the most comfortable. At the Retiro weather station, average highs are 22 °C in May and 19 °C in October, against 32 °C in July and 31 °C in August. January highs average 10 °C, with lows near 3 °C (AEMET 1981–2010 normals, [AEMET](https://www.aemet.es/en/serviciosclimaticos/datosclimatologicos/valoresclimatologicos?l=3195&k=mad)).
