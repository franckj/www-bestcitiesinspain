Spain has more great cities than one trip can hold. These eight cover the big sights, the food and the coast, and all of them are on direct train lines from Madrid. Three hold UNESCO World Heritage sites: the [Alhambra](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/314/) in Granada, the [cathedral and Alcázar](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/383/) in Seville, and the [works of Antoni Gaudí](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/320/) in Barcelona.

## How to choose

- **One week:** Madrid or Barcelona, then Seville and Granada. See the [7-day plan](/itineraries/).
- **Art first:** Madrid for the Prado, Bilbao for the Guggenheim, Málaga for Picasso.
- **Food first:** San Sebastián for pintxos; Valencia, home of [paella](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paella).
- **The sea:** Barcelona, Valencia, Málaga and San Sebastián all have a city beach.
- **Summer trip:** go north. Bilbao and San Sebastián average highs of about 25–26°C in July and August, against 36°C in Seville ([best time to visit](/best-time-to-visit-spain/)).

Book the big tickets before you go: the Alhambra, the Sagrada Família and the Real Alcázar all sell tickets online, and the Alhambra gives you a fixed slot for the Nasrid Palaces. See [Spain tours and tickets](/spain-tours/).
