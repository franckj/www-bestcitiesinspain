## The three train companies

- **Renfe**, the state operator, runs every route on this page. Its high-speed brands are **AVE** (standard), **Avlo** (low-cost, fewer extras) and **Alvia** (trains that also run on conventional track, used to Bilbao and San Sebastián). **Avant** trains are shorter regional high-speed runs, such as Seville–Granada. Since 8 September 2025, Renfe has run AVE instead of Avlo between Madrid and Barcelona ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avlo)).
- **Iryo**, part-owned by Italy's Trenitalia, runs Madrid–Barcelona, Madrid–Valencia and Madrid–Córdoba–Seville/Málaga ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iryo)). Its site blocked our checks, so we couldn't confirm its current timetable.
- **Ouigo**, owned by France's SNCF, runs low-cost double-deck [TGV Euroduplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ouigo_Espa%C3%B1a) trains. Its [route page](https://www.ouigo.com/es/en/destinations) lists Madrid–Barcelona, Madrid–Valencia, Madrid–Seville and Málaga–Madrid. Ouigo gives an average of 2 h 47 for Madrid–Seville, from Atocha to Santa Justa ([Ouigo](https://www.ouigo.com/es/tren-tren-madrid-sevilla-barato), checked 14 October 2026).

The three companies share the same high-speed lines, so journey times are close. Compare prices across all three before you book.

## Madrid: Atocha or Chamartín?

Madrid has two main long-distance stations, and the wrong one costs you a train.

- **Madrid Puerta de Atocha – Almudena Grandes:** trains to Barcelona, Seville, Córdoba, Málaga and Granada. It is on the south side of the city centre.
- **Madrid Chamartín – Clara Campoamor:** trains to Valencia (13 of 15 Renfe trains on our sample day), Bilbao and San Sebastián. It is in the north of the city.

Station names are as Renfe lists them in its timetable data.

## Routes in detail

### Madrid – Barcelona

Direct AVE trains run from Atocha to Barcelona Sants from about 06:16 to 20:30. On 21 October 2026, the fastest took 3 h 02 (for example 06:57 → 09:59), and others took up to 3 h 47 with more stops. Some trains stop at Zaragoza and Camp de Tarragona. Iryo and Ouigo also run this route.

### Madrid – Seville and Madrid – Málaga

Both run from Atocha via Córdoba. Madrid–Seville took 2 h 38 to 3 h 09 (18 Renfe trains), and Madrid–Málaga 2 h 44 to 3 h 15 (12 Renfe trains). Madrid–Córdoba alone takes about 1 h 45 if you want a stop on the way.

### Madrid – Valencia

The quickest hop: 1 h 54 to 2 h 05, mostly from Chamartín to València Joaquín Sorolla. Renfe, Iryo and Ouigo all run it.

### Madrid – Granada

Four direct Renfe trains on our sample day: 07:20, 13:35, 16:40 and 19:50 from Atocha, taking 3 h 23 to 3 h 41.

### Seville – Granada

There's a direct train now: four Renfe Avant trains (07:40, 09:55, 12:17 and 19:15 from Santa Justa on 21 October 2026), taking 2 h 37 to 2 h 45. The ALSA bus is the back-up: 12 a day, 3 to 5 hours ([ALSA](https://www.alsa.com/es/ruta/granada-sevilla), checked 14 October 2026).

### Granada – Málaga and Málaga – Seville

Three Avant trains a day link Granada and Málaga María Zambrano in about 1 h 30. Málaga to Seville takes 2 h 07 to 2 h 22 on the six Avant trains, or about 3 h 15 to 3 h 30 on the slower MD regional trains.

### Madrid – Bilbao and Madrid – San Sebastián

These are the long ones. Two Alvia trains a day ran from Chamartín to Bilbao Abando (06:55 and 17:48, about 5 h 10), and three trains to San Sebastián-Donostia (about 5 h 25 to 5 h 37). From Bilbao to San Sebastián, take the ALSA bus (1 h 10 to 1 h 30, 12 a day).

### Barcelona – Valencia

Renfe's Euromed trains run Barcelona Sants to València Estació del Nord in 2 h 47 to 3 h 26 (six on our sample day). Slower Alvia, Intercity and regional trains also run.

## When a flight makes sense

Some routes are long by train: Barcelona–Seville took 6 h 17 at best (two direct trains), and Barcelona–Bilbao 7 h 03 (one train) on 21 October 2026. Vueling flies direct between Barcelona and both Seville and Bilbao (route lists on Wikipedia for [Seville Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seville_Airport) and [Bilbao Airport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilbao_Airport)). Add the time to get to and through the airport before you choose.
