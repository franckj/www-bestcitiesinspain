## Month by month

| Months | What to expect | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| January–February | Cool in Madrid and Granada (highs 10–15°C, frosty nights in Granada); mild on the coast (Málaga and Valencia about 17°C) | Quiet and cheaper; good for Málaga, Valencia, Seville |
| March | Las Fallas in Valencia, 15–19 March; Easter week 21–28 March in 2027 | Good, but book Valencia and Seville early |
| April | Seville's April Fair, two weeks after Holy Week; highs of 18–24°C | Very good, busy in Seville |
| May–June | Warm everywhere; highs about 28–33°C in Seville | Best months for most of Spain |
| July–August | Hot inland: average highs 32–36°C in Seville, Madrid and Granada; mild on the Basque coast | Go north, or plan early starts |
| September | Still warm; San Sebastián Film Festival | Very good |
| October | Highs of 23–26°C in Seville, Granada and Málaga; rain returns | Very good |
| November–December | Wettest months in the north; mild in Málaga | Quiet; good for the Mediterranean cities |

Temperatures are AEMET averages, 1991–2020, from the climate tables in each city's Wikipedia article. Details below.

## The south: hot summers, mild winters

Seville, Granada and Málaga are in Andalusia.

- **Seville** is the hottest: average highs of 36.3°C in July and 36.2°C in August. It's also one of the mildest in winter (16.3°C in January). It barely rains in summer (0.1 rainy days in July).
- **Granada** sits inland and high: the airport weather station is at 567 m. Summers are hotter than Madrid's (34.8°C in July), and winter nights are cold: the average January low there is 0.3°C. In summer, an early Alhambra slot avoids the afternoon heat.
- **Málaga** is on the coast. It has the warmest winter of the eight (17.2°C in January) and a hot, dry summer (31.0°C in July).

## Madrid and the centre

Madrid is at 667 m. It has the biggest gap between seasons: 10.0°C average high in January, 32.8°C in July. October is the rainiest month (7.2 rainy days). Spring and autumn are the best times.

## The Mediterranean coast

**Barcelona** and **Valencia** have mild winters and warm, humid summers. Highs in July are 28.3°C in Barcelona and 29.9°C in Valencia, lower than inland. September and October bring the year's heaviest rain in both cities (86 mm in September at Barcelona airport, 70 mm in Valencia).

## The Basque coast: green and wet

**San Sebastián** and **Bilbao** are the coolest and wettest of the eight. July highs are about 25°C, which makes them a good summer escape. But it rains often in every month: about 10 rainy days even in July in San Sebastián, and 14 in November. Pack a rain jacket whatever the season.

## Festivals and events

- **Las Fallas, Valencia: 15–19 March every year.** Giant figures go up across the city and are burned on the last night. The *mascletà* firecracker shows run daily from 1 to 19 March ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falles)). UNESCO added it to its intangible heritage list in 2016.
- **Semana Santa (Easter week): 21–28 March 2027.** Easter Sunday 2027 falls on 28 March. Seville's processions are the best known, with nearly 70 brotherhoods ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Week_in_Seville)).
- **Feria de Abril, Seville.** The April Fair generally begins two weeks after Holy Week and runs six days, Monday to Sunday ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seville_Fair)). We couldn't find official 2027 dates from Seville's city council when we checked on 14 October 2026.
- **San Sebastián International Film Festival:** generally in September ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Sebasti%C3%A1n_International_Film_Festival)). Check the festival's site for exact dates.

## Which cities to pair by season

| Season | Good pairing | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Spring (April–June) | Madrid, Seville, Granada | Warm, before the summer heat |
| Summer (July–August) | San Sebastián, Bilbao, Barcelona | Coolest highs of the eight (25–28°C) |
| Autumn (September–October) | Any; Andalusia is at its best | Highs of 23–26°C in Seville, Granada and Málaga in October |
| Winter (November–February) | Málaga, Seville, Valencia | Mildest January highs (16–17°C) |
