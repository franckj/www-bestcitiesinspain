## Book these three first

| Ticket | Where to book | Price (checked 14 Oct 2026) | Good to know |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alhambra, Granada | [Official site](https://tickets.alhambra-patronato.es/en/) | €22.27 general | Fixed Nasrid Palaces slot; bring the ID on the ticket |
| Sagrada Família, Barcelona | [Official site](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/tickets-individuals) | From €26 | Guided tour and tower options up to €40 |
| Real Alcázar, Seville | [Official site](https://alcazardesevilla.infoticketing.com/es) | €15.50 general | Upper royal rooms €5.50 extra |

A guided tour that includes the ticket is the other way in when you can't get an official slot. Check what each tour includes before you book.

## How to choose a tour

- **First day in a city:** a walking tour gives you the layout and the history.
- **Big sights:** a guided tour of the Alhambra, the Sagrada Família, the Real Alcázar or the Prado includes the ticket and skips the planning.
- **Food:** tapas and pintxo tours, with the tastings included.
- **Day trips:** tours from the bigger cities to nearby towns.
