## 7 days: Madrid, Seville and Granada

| Days | Base | Getting there |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | [Madrid](/destinations/madrid/) | Fly into Madrid |
| 4–5 | [Seville](/destinations/seville/) | Atocha → Santa Justa, 2 h 38 to 3 h 09 |
| 6–7 | [Granada](/destinations/granada/) | Santa Justa → Granada, 2 h 37 to 2 h 45 (4 Avant trains a day) |

**Day 1–3, Madrid.** The Prado and the Paseo del Prado, the Royal Palace, the Retiro. Book the Prado and the Royal Palace ahead if you can.

**Day 4–5, Seville.** Take a morning AVE from Atocha. Book the Real Alcázar before you go: tickets are sold on its [official site](https://alcazardesevilla.infoticketing.com/es) (general entry €15.50, checked 14 October 2026). Add the cathedral and an evening in Triana.

**Day 6–7, Granada.** Take the direct Avant train (07:40, 09:55, 12:17 or 19:15 from Santa Justa on our sample day). Book the Alhambra weeks ahead: the ticket gives you a fixed half-hour slot for the Nasrid Palaces ([Alhambra FAQ](https://www.alhambra-patronato.es/en/visit/faq)). Spend the second day in the Albaicín.

**Getting home.** Direct trains run from Granada to Madrid Atocha (3 h 27 to 3 h 48, four on our sample day), or fly from Granada or Málaga. Granada to Málaga is about 1 h 30 by Avant train.

## 10 days: Barcelona, Madrid, Seville and Granada

| Days | Base | Getting there |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | [Barcelona](/destinations/barcelona/) | Fly into Barcelona |
| 4–6 | [Madrid](/destinations/madrid/) | Sants → Atocha, 3 h 02 to 3 h 50 |
| 7–8 | [Seville](/destinations/seville/) | Atocha → Santa Justa, 2 h 38 to 3 h 09 |
| 9–10 | [Granada](/destinations/granada/) | Santa Justa → Granada, 2 h 37 to 2 h 45 |

This is the 7-day plan with Barcelona in front.

**Day 1–3, Barcelona.** The Sagrada Família (book on the [official site](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/tickets-individuals): from €26, checked 14 October 2026), Gaudí's other buildings, the Gothic Quarter and the beach.

**Day 4–6, Madrid.** The Madrid–Barcelona line is the busiest: Renfe ran 25 direct trains on our sample day, and Iryo and Ouigo add more.

**Day 7–10.** As in the 7-day plan: Seville, then Granada. Fly home from Granada or Málaga, or take the train back to Madrid.

**Short on time?** Drop Granada and do Barcelona (3), Madrid (3), Seville (3), then fly home from Seville.

## 14 days: two ways

### Option A: add Valencia and Málaga

| Days | Base | Getting there |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Barcelona | Fly in |
| 4–5 | [Valencia](/destinations/valencia/) | Sants → Estació del Nord, 2 h 47 to 3 h 26 (Euromed) |
| 6–8 | Madrid | Joaquín Sorolla → Chamartín, 1 h 56 to 2 h 10 |
| 9–10 | Seville | Atocha → Santa Justa, 2 h 38 to 3 h 09 |
| 11–12 | Granada | Santa Justa → Granada, 2 h 37 to 2 h 45 |
| 13–14 | [Málaga](/destinations/malaga/) | Granada → María Zambrano, about 1 h 30 (Avant, 3 a day) |

Fly home from Málaga. Note the two Valencia stations: trains from Barcelona arrive at Estació del Nord, while most trains to Madrid leave from Joaquín Sorolla.

### Option B: the south and the Basque Country

| Days | Base | Getting there |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Madrid | Fly in |
| 4–5 | Seville | Atocha → Santa Justa, 2 h 38 to 3 h 09 |
| 6–7 | Granada | Santa Justa → Granada, 2 h 37 to 2 h 45 |
| 8 | Back to Madrid | Granada → Atocha, 3 h 27 to 3 h 48 |
| 9–11 | [San Sebastián](/destinations/san-sebastian/) | Chamartín → San Sebastián-Donostia, 5 h 25 to 5 h 37 (3 a day) |
| 12–14 | [Bilbao](/destinations/bilbao/) | ALSA bus, 1 h 10 to 1 h 30 (12 a day) |

Fly home from Bilbao. Day 8 is a travel day; spend the evening in Madrid. If you'd rather save the time, fly from Seville or Málaga to Bilbao and do the Basque Country in reverse. We haven't checked those flight schedules, so look them up first.

## Which plan fits you

| You are… | Do this |
|---|---|
| On your first trip, one week | 7 days: Madrid, Seville, Granada |
| Flying into Barcelona | 10 days, ending in Granada or Málaga |
| Travelling in July or August | Option B, or spend more time in the north: Bilbao averages 25.4°C in July against 36.3°C in Seville ([best time to visit](/best-time-to-visit-spain/)) |
| Here for food | Option B, for San Sebastián's pintxo bars |
| Coming at Easter | Book Seville first: Holy Week 2027 runs 21–28 March |
